About Anime Higher or Lower

Anime Higher or Lower is a free anime guessing game. Two titles face off and you guess which one is more popular: pick higher or lower, keep the streak going and beat your best score. A new daily challenge is the same for everyone.

Modes: Anime (the 1,000 most popular anime, by members or favorites), Romance (the 400 most popular romance anime), Characters (which character has more fans) and Hair Color, where you paint a character’s hair as close to the real color as you can.

All titles, numbers and images come from AniList and are refreshed every week.
