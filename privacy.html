Privacy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version

No accounts, no cookies, no ads. We count visits anonymously to see how the game is doing, and your scores never leave your device.

Anonymous statistics

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t track you across websites and doesn’t collect personal information. It records:

  • Page visits, with the referring site, browser, operating system, device type and country.
  • A few game events: when a game starts and ends (game mode and score) and when you tap “Share result”.

This data is aggregated and can’t be used to identify you. If you block the Umami script, the game works exactly the same.

Stored on your device

Your best scores and daily challenge results are saved in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes them.

Anime data and images

Titles, numbers, cover art and character images come from AniList. Images load directly from AniList’s servers, so your browser connects to them as it would on any website that shows their images. All artwork belongs to its respective owners.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs for security.
